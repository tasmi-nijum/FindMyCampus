FindMyCampus - Peer Testing and Improvements
During peer testing, another group tested our Campus Guide website.
Tested by:
Date: 7 November 2026

Feedback 1:
The purpose of our website was clear. Users could easily understand that it helps students navigate around the campus.

Feedback 2:
The website design and navigation were good. The navigation links worked correctly.

Feedback 3:
The responsive design worked well on different screen sizes, including Samsung and iPhone devices.


Feedback 4:
The tester said that the demo video was difficult to find because it was placed near the bottom of the website.

Improvement 1:
We added a demo video link to make it easier for users to find the video.

Feedback 5:
The tester had difficulty finding and playing the audio.

Improvement 2:
We checked the audio button and tested the audio playback.
